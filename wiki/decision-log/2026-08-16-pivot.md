## 2026-08-15 — STRATEGIC PIVOT: SaaS agency parked, Game Studio Hermes established

**Decision:** Park the SaaS portfolio and pivot the operation to a game studio.
The 5 SaaS products (NoMarkup, CrossState, AgentScope, ApproveGuard, SandboxAudit)
are archived, not deleted. New operation lives in a separate repo
(`sxaad69/hermes-game-studio`, public) with its own board, doctrine, and skills.

**Why:** Post-mortem of the SaaS portfolio (see prior analysis):
- Distribution was the structural gap — an agent-run SaaS has no credible general
  distribution funnel (trust/brand/sales are human assets agents cannot create).
- 4 of 5 products clustered in the AI-dev echo chamber (research method bias:
  HN/Reddit/GitHub scan produces dev-tool ideas). Zero users, zero revenue, M2-M3.
- Games + web portals (CrazyGames/Poki/itch) invert this: the deliverable is the
  work product (playable HTML5 game), distribution is portal-native (submit →
  metric gate → monetize), no trust deficit, every step agent-executable except
  the final human "fun" verdict.

**Category:** Procedural casual puzzle first (~60%), physics one-button second
(~30%), experiments (10%). Rationale: puzzle = agent superpower (procedural level
generation + automated solvability verification), matches older/higher-retention
demo, evergreen shelf life. Portal gates: 10+ min avg playtime, 10-15% D1
retention, 80%+ conversion, <20MB build, PEGI12, originality enforced by human QA.

**Assets:** Hybrid stack — Kenney CC0 (structural) + AI-generated hero assets
(originality) + jsfxr procedural SFX. `assets.json` provenance manifest per game.

**Stack:** Godot 4.x → headless web export on the VPS → nginx serve → headless
Chromium smoke-playtest → portal submission (Basic Launch → Full Launch, ~60% ad
share). Export ladder later: PWA, Android.

**Ops changes:**
- SaaS boards archived (`default` kept as system board; `sprint-1`, `agency-ops`
  → `_archived/`). SaaS systemd services stopped + disabled on the VPS.
- Mac-side agency install fully dormant (crons paused, watchdog unloaded,
  SaaS launchd agents disabled). VPS is the single operator.
- 3-worker hard cap + no-local-models doctrine carry over unchanged.
- New `games` board; research retargeted to the 6-topic game research program.

**Outcome expected:** 2-3 games shipped to a playtest URL first (curated skill
set), then expand to the full gamedev skill catalogs. Kill-first doctrine applies
to every concept before any build card.
